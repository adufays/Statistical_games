Empirical Exercise II  ·  Multivariate Data Analysis

Master Scepticus does his shopping at Monoprix, next door, and claims that it costs no more than Carrefour. The prices of 20 products in both shops will judge him. You will draw a scatter plot, estimate a simple linear regression, read its coefficients, and use it to predict prices, once wisely and once not.

WINDOW 1 · EXCELDownload Monoprix_Carrefour.xlsx with the DATA (XLSX) button at the top and open it in Excel. Compute every number there. Keep your workbook: each question builds on the previous one.
WINDOW 2 · THIS GAMEMaster Scepticus asks the questions, one after the other. Bring back the numbers, choose the interpretations, and defend them in front of him. The game never computes anything for you.

No answer is ever shown in this exercise. Every question has a HINT button, with an example Excel formula, sometimes with ??? for you to fill in. Keep trying until the Master accepts your answer.

Checkpoints. Five times during the exercise, your progress is saved. The latest checkpoint always stays at the top of your notebook. If you close this window, you can resume from your last checkpoint on the same computer.

Type numbers with a dot or a comma as the decimal separator, e.g., 4.02 or 4,02. Give prices in euros to the cent, e.g., 4.02, and the other numbers with three decimals, e.g., 0.984.
