Empirical Exercise III  ·  Multivariate Data Analysis

Master Scepticus will hand you the training compute of frontier AI models and a straight line that predicts negative compute. You will fit your first regressions, replace the line with a log-linear model, make a conditional prediction for 2029, and say out loud what that extrapolation assumes.

WINDOW 1 · EXCELDownload Frontier_AI_compute.xlsx with the DATA button at the top and open it in Excel. Compute every number there, and keep your workbook: each question builds on the previous one.
WINDOW 2 · THIS GAMEMaster Scepticus asks the questions, one after the other. Bring back the numbers, choose the interpretations, and defend them in front of him. The game never computes anything for you.

No answer is ever shown in this exercise. Every question has a HINT button, with an example Excel formula, sometimes with ??? for you to fill in. Keep trying until the Master accepts your answer.

Checkpoints. Five times during the exercise, the Master gives you a password. The latest one always stays at the top of your notebook. If you cannot finish, submit the latest password on Blackboard: it shows how far you got. If you close this window, you can resume from your last checkpoint on the same computer.

Type numbers with a dot or a comma as the decimal separator, e.g., 0.12 or 0,12. Two decimals are enough. Very large numbers can be typed exactly as Excel displays them, e.g., -4.76E+25.
