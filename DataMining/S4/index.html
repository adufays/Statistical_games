Empirical Exercise IV  ·  Data Mining

The Academy sells its old fleet, and Master Scepticus wants a machine that predicts the price of a used Mercedes. He is sure that the simplest model predicts best: the price, explained by the mileage alone. 13,119 cars will test him. You will estimate two linear regressions of the price, compare their predictions out of sample with leave-one-out and 10-fold cross-validation, and then try a third model, on the logarithm of the price.
