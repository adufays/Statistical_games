Master Scepticus believes that one z-score catches every cheat. 505 simulated positive numbers, five of them rogue, will test him. You will detect outliers with the standard deviation and with the MAD, on the raw data and on their logarithm. Then you will check their first digits against Benford’s law with the z-score of a proportion.
