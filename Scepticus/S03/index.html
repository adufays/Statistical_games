Exercise III  ·  Statistical Models

You will compare self-reported and measured height and weight, formulate directional hypotheses, estimate sampling uncertainty from the data, and carry out approximate Normal tests.

Because the variance is estimated, an exact small-sample analysis under Normal sampling would use Student's t distribution. Here we deliberately use the standard Normal distribution as an approximation and discuss why that approximation improves with sample size.
